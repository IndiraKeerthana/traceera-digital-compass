## What Is Traceera?

Every photo you post, every old account you forget, every data breach you never hear about — they all leave a trail.

**Traceera** helps users understand that trail.

It scans publicly available information linked to an email, username, or phone number and turns it into a simple, easy-to-understand **Safety Score** that reflects how exposed someone is online.

> **Think of it as a credit score for your digital privacy.**

---

## The Problem

Most people have no idea:

* How much of their personal information is public
* Whether their email has appeared in data breaches
* How easy it is for someone to profile them
* Which accounts are increasing their privacy risk

Traceera makes that invisible risk visible.

---

## What Traceera Does

A user enters:

* 📧 Email address
* 📱 Phone number
* 👤 Username or handle

Traceera analyzes:

* Known data breaches
* Public social profiles
* Search engine results
* Metadata exposure
* Privacy misconfigurations

And generates:

* 🔐 Safety Score (0–100)
* 📊 Risk breakdown
* 🧠 Personalized recommendations
* 📈 Privacy improvement tips

---

## 🖥️ Sample Output

```text
Safety Score: 63/100

⚠️ Risks Detected
- Email found in 4 data breaches
- Instagram account is public
- Phone number indexed online
- Same username used across multiple platforms

✅ Recommendations
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Change compromised passwords
- Remove public phone number
- Tighten social media privacy settings
```

## Why We Built This

We wanted to create something that solves a real-world problem.

Cybersecurity tools are often designed for professionals, but everyday users also deserve to know how exposed they are online.

Traceera translates technical findings into a score and recommendations anyone can understand.
